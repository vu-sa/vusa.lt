# Platform updates: v3

## v3.10 — Responsibilities instead of roles (2026-10-19) {#v3-10}

Roles became **responsibilities**: a responsibility says what a duty does and grants the permissions
that work needs. Every former role was kept with the same permissions, so nobody's access changed. A
responsibility can be given to a duty, a duty type or, rarely, a whole institution.

Guide: [Responsibilities](/pagrindai/atsakomybes), [Responsibilities and permissions](/sistema/roles-ir-leidimai) · My VU SR: [Responsibilities](app:/mano/responsibilities)

### Other changes and fixes {#v3-10-kiti}

- ✨ **Duty type responsibilities** – grants and their scope are managed on the responsibility page; the type record shows their list and links. Assigning types requires delegation and access to the affected duty.
  Guide: [Duty types](/sistema/tipai#pareigybes-tipo-atsakomybes)

- ✨ **Permission grid** – all of a responsibility's permissions are seen and changed in one table grouped by workspace, with one “Save” and a summary of changes. Permissions for mailing lists, goals, study sets and event types now appear in it too.
  Guide: [Setting permissions](/sistema/roles-ir-leidimai#veiksmai)
- ⭐ **Title and description** – a responsibility has a Lithuanian and English title and description.
  Guide: [Creating a responsibility](/sistema/roles-ir-leidimai#veiksmai)
- ✨ **Given to and held by** – the responsibility page shows the duties, types and institutions it is given to and who holds it now. The duty page also shows responsibilities it gets through its type or institution.
  Guide: [Giving a responsibility](/sistema/roles-ir-leidimai#veiksmai)
- ⭐ **System purposes** – settings say which responsibility counts as student representation, coordination and member registration, and which types appear in contacts as coordinators, curators and mentors.
  Guide: [System purposes](/sistema/nustatymai#sistemos-paskirtys) · My VU SR: [Settings](app:/mano/settings)
- ✨ **Member registrations** – registrations go to and are visible for duties holding the “Member registration” responsibility; form settings show where nobody receives them.
  Guide: [Form settings](/sistema/nustatymai#formos)
- ⭐ **Mailing lists by responsibility** – a mailing list can take its recipients by responsibility, e.g. every student representative coordinator.
  Guide: [Recipient sources](/organizacija/konferencijos)
- 🔧 **Safer granting** – a coordinator editing a duty can only give responsibilities they hold themselves; the duty form no longer changes responsibilities.
  Guide: [Who can do what](/sistema/roles-ir-leidimai#teises)

## v3.9 — Recently ended check-ins (2026-10-19) {#v3-9}

- 🔧 **Banner previews** – thumbnails and image previews work as soon as the banner list opens.
  Guide: [Banner list](/svetaine/baneriai#baneriu-sarasas-ir-greitas-busenos-keitimas) · My VU SR: [Banners](app:/mano/banners)

- ⭐ **Sort the timeline and group it by next meeting** – institutions can be sorted by name, last activity, next or last meeting, and grouped into “This week”, “Next week”, “This month”, “Later”, “Past activity” and “No information”. The date beside each name shows what the list is sorted by. The legend moved next to the institution count.
  Guide: [Meetings timeline](/visak/padaliniai#posedziu-laiko-juosta) · My VU SR: [Units](app:/mano/dashboard/atstovavimas/padaliniai)
- ✨ **Check-in recently ended** – an ended no-meeting check-in has its own status for half the institution’s interval. Overviews and the trend chart show these institutions separately, with the check-in end date explaining the status.
  Guide: [Institution status](/visak/apzvalga#busenos)
- ✨ **Ask about activity sooner** – coordinators can send “Did a meeting take place?” after a check-in ends, with its date visible before sending. Automatic questions wait until overdue; requests to complete meeting records remain separate.
  Guide: [Requests to representatives](/visak/institucijos#paklausti)
- 🔧 **Automatic requests in one email** – automatic “Did a meeting take place?” and meeting-record requests now reach each representative in one email covering all their institutions, as a coordinator's send does, instead of one email per institution.
  Guide: [Requests to representatives](/visak/institucijos#paklausti)
- ✨ **Automatic request once a month** – an institution is asked about automatically at most once a month after its last request, even an expired one or one answered “I do not represent this institution”. Coordinators can ask at any time.
  Guide: [Requests to representatives](/visak/institucijos#paklausti)
- 🔧 **Automatic request reaches new representatives** – when an institution's representatives change, the open “Report activity” task and its request go to the current representatives instead of former ones.
  Guide: [Automatic tasks](/mano/uzduotys#automatines)
- 🔧 **A partial answer no longer closes colleagues' requests** – when one representative confirms only part of the period (e.g. from their own start), colleagues' “Did a meeting take place?” requests stay open so they can report the rest.
  Guide: [When an institution has several representatives](/visak/institucijos#keli-atstovai)
- 🔧 **Institutions shown on the coordinator card** – the ViSAK overview once again lists the names of the institutions each coordinator covers, instead of empty commas.
  Guide: [Coordinator](/visak/#koordinatorius) · My VU SR: [ViSAK](app:/mano/dashboard/atstovavimas)
- ✨ **Automatic tasks without a separate notice** – a new meeting no longer sends the representative “New task” notices about its agenda. The meeting is announced once with “New meeting created”, the task appears in Tasks, and reminders arrive as the deadline approaches. Tasks assigned by a person still notify as before.
  Guide: [Who gets notified and when](/pagrindai/pranesimai#auditorija) · My VU SR: [Notification settings](app:/mano/profile/notifications)
- 🔧 **Agenda items of deleted meetings in search** – once a meeting is deleted, its agenda items no longer show up in search with links that do not open. Restoring the meeting makes them findable again.
  Guide: [Deleting agenda items](/visak/darbotvarkes-klausimai#istrynimas)
- 🔧 **Contacts** – study programme committees and other institutions that shared one address (e.g. `padalinio-studentu-atstovai-8`) now have one each, so a card opens the institution you chose. Old addresses redirect to the new ones, except shared ones, generated ones (e.g. `pkp-105`) and ones with spaces or Lithuanian letters – these no longer work.
  Guide: [Public address](/visak/institucijos#adresas)
- ✨ **Institution form** – renaming an institution changes its address too; earlier addresses are listed under “Previous public addresses”.
  Guide: [Public address](/visak/institucijos#adresas) · My VU SR: [Institutions](app:/mano/institutions)

## v3.8 — Meetings timeline updates and activity request logic improvements (2026-10-18) {#v3-8}

The meetings timeline can be grouped by institution type, shows unanswered requests to representatives, and loads meetings right to the edges of the screen when zoomed out.

- ⭐ **Grouping by institution type** – the toolbar's **Group** button arranges institutions by unit, by type, or in one list. An institution with several types appears under each of them. This setting moved here from the filters.
  Guide: [Meetings timeline](/visak/padaliniai#posedziu-laiko-juosta) · My VU SR: [Units](app:/mano/dashboard/atstovavimas/padaliniai)

- ⭐ **Requests to representatives on the timeline** – a “Did a meeting take place?” request is shown on the institution's row across the period it asks about, and “Complete meeting records” marks the incomplete meetings. Coordinators see every unanswered request, representatives only the ones sent to them. They can be hidden in the filters.
  Guide: [Meetings timeline](/visak/padaliniai#posedziu-laiko-juosta)

- ✨ **The timeline follows the statistics' units** – changing the units at the top of the page shows the same units on the timeline. Its units are picked with a separate **Showing** button, and the filters keep only display settings. The “Detailed rows” button was removed.
  Guide: [Meetings timeline](/visak/padaliniai#posedziu-laiko-juosta)

- ✨ **Finer zoom** – the zoom moves in smaller steps, especially when zoomed out.

- 🔧 **Timeline loading** – when zoomed out, meetings load across the whole screen, not only its middle; changing units no longer reloads the timeline from scratch. The institution column no longer stays stuck to the pointer after the mouse button is released.
  Guide: [Meetings timeline](/visak/padaliniai#posedziu-laiko-juosta)

- ✨ **Duty types** – filter the duties collection by a type or choose **No type**.
  Guide: [Duties](/organizacija/pareigybes#tipai) · My VU SR: [Duties](app:/mano/duties)
- 🔧 **Request recipients** – the request preview now explains when an institution has no duties with the “Student representative” type.
  Guide: [Requests to representatives](/visak/institucijos#paklausti)
- 🔧 **“Did a meeting take place?” waits until the interval is exceeded** – coordinator requests follow the same conditions as automatic requests, accounting for recent activity, vacations, upcoming meetings, and active no-meeting reports. An open request is no longer sent again the next day.
  Guide: [Requests to representatives](/visak/institucijos#paklausti)

- ✨ **Institutions without activity records** – the first request becomes due based on the recipient’s service start in the current term. Requests to complete older meeting records remain available during an active no-meeting report.
  Guide: [Institutions](/visak/institucijos#paklausti), [Automatic tasks](/mano/uzduotys#automatines)

## v3.7 — Mailing lists (2026-10-18) {#v3-7}

### Mailing lists in Organisation {#v3-7-konferencijos}

My VU SR now includes `konf.vusa.lt` mailing lists, their recipients, membership sources and
message archives. Automatically managed lists follow duties currently in force; manually
managed lists continue to be maintained in Mailman.

Before enabling automatic management, coordinators can review additions and removals. Archive
history imports in batches, with older messages accessible from the same list page.

Guide: [Mailing lists](/organizacija/konferencijos) · My VU SR: [Mailing lists](app:/mano/mailingLists)

  within their authorized units; central-office coordinators can manage all lists.
  Guide: [Who can do what](/organizacija/konferencijos#kas-ka-gali) · My VU SR: [Mailing lists](app:/mano/mailingLists)

### Other changes and fixes {#v3-7-kiti}

- ✨ **Mailing list connection status** — system status shows Mailman and archive connectivity,
  together with lists whose recipient updates need attention.
  Guide: [System status](/sistema/sistemos-busena) · My VU SR: [System status](app:/mano/system-status)

## v3.4 — More complete member and duty merging (2026-10-18) {#v3-4}

- ⭐ **Possible duplicates** – filter the members list for profiles with matching names while keeping existing access restrictions for other units. Returning from a member profile keeps the filter and matching results. Guide: [Member list and filters](/organizacija/nariai#sarasas) · My VU SR: [Members](app:/mano/users)
- ✨ **Member merging** – several records for the same person can be merged at once, with a choice of which email address to keep for signing in. The editing form shows possible duplicates for the same person. Guide: [Merging members](/organizacija/nariai#sujungimas) · My VU SR: [Members](app:/mano/users)
- ✨ **Duty contacts** – the merge dialog lets you preserve an old email address on selected duty periods. Source profile photos are automatically saved on their duty periods, keeping existing duty photos. Guide: [Merging members](/organizacija/nariai#sujungimas)
- 🔧 **Activity history** – merging preserves comments, reservations, tasks, the profile photo and staged images used by content drafts. Overlapping duty periods are combined, while separate periods remain separate. Guide: [Merging members](/organizacija/nariai#sujungimas)
- 🔧 **Merge safeguards** – names are checked for a match. If the kept account’s email cannot be changed, its current login address stays. Super administrators can also merge records with differing names; a source account with directly assigned roles or permissions cannot be merged. Guide: [Merging members](/organizacija/nariai#sujungimas)
- ⭐ **Possible duplicate duties** – the duties list can show duties in the same institution whose names match, e.g. "Koordinatorius" and "Koordinatorė". In the duty form's warning, "Merge into this one" opens the merge dialog with both duties. Guide: [Duty list and filters](/organizacija/pareigybes#sarasas) · My VU SR: [Duties](app:/mano/duties)
- ✨ **Duty merging** – only super administrators can merge duties. Merged duties are deleted permanently instead of being moved to the trash. Guide: [Merging duties](/organizacija/pareigybes#sujungimas) · My VU SR: [Duties](app:/mano/duties)
- 🔧 **What moves when duties are merged** – responsibilities, goals, comments, files, tasks and directly assigned permissions now also move to the kept duty, and its members get the moved roles' access straight away. Guide: [Merging duties](/organizacija/pareigybes#sujungimas)
- 🔧 **Name changes** – members can no longer change their own name in their profile; super administrators do it instead. Names that members had already changed themselves are marked with an asterisk (*) for review. Guide: [Updating your details](/mano/paskyra-ir-prieiga#profilis) · My VU SR: [Profile](app:/mano/profile)
- 🔧 **All members** – administrators with global member-viewing access can also see accounts with no duty history or directly assigned roles. Guide: [Members without duty history](/organizacija/nariai#padaliniai-ir-teises)

## v3.3 — Shared accessibility settings (2026-10-18) {#v3-3}

### Accessibility settings in your profile {#v3-3-prieinamumas}

Text size, contrast, link underlining and reduced motion can now also be adjusted in your
profile. The same settings apply to My VU SR and the public website on the same domain.

With reduced motion, slides and cards stop changing automatically, and page transitions and
scrolling happen without animation. Your operating system's reduced motion preference is also respected.

Guide: [Accessibility settings](/mano/paskyra-ir-prieiga#prieinamumas) · My VU SR: [Profile](app:/mano/profile)

### Other changes and fixes {#v3-3-kiti}

- ✨ **Keyboard navigation** – tables can be sorted using buttons, search inputs have clear labels, and a skip link leads past the admin navigation to the main content. Closing accessibility settings returns keyboard focus to the button that opened them. Guide: [Accessibility settings](/mano/paskyra-ir-prieiga#prieinamumas)
- 🔧 **Mobile menu** – **Escape** closes an open language or accessibility popup before closing the menu itself. The open menu stops the page from scrolling and closes when switching to the desktop layout. Guide: [Accessibility settings](/mano/paskyra-ir-prieiga#prieinamumas)

## v3.2 — Fewer “Did a meeting take place?” questions (2026-10-18) {#v3-2}

Representatives get fewer repeated questions about their institution's meetings.

- 🔧 **Did a meeting take place?** – once a “No meeting” report ends, the same question is no longer sent again the next morning. Guide: [Notifications and automation](/visak/institucijos#pranesimai)
- ✨ **When you are asked** – the automatic email is sent once, at 10:00, when the institution becomes overdue. The “Report activity” task still appears as the deadline approaches, but it no longer sends reminders 7, 3 and 1 days before. Guide: [Tasks](/mano/uzduotys#pranesimai)
- ✨ **Which period you are asked about** – the period starts after the last meeting or the previous report, when these are later than the start of the term. Guide: [Ask representatives](/visak/institucijos#paklausti)
- ✨ **Institution status** – “Contact recorded” is now “Activity reported”, as on the ViSAK overview. Guide: [Statuses](/visak/apzvalga#busenos)
- ✨ **No, it did not** – when answering from the email, you can say there will be no meetings for two more weeks, until the end of the month or until a date you choose. Guide: [Answering from the email](/visak/institucijos#atsakymas)

## v3.1 — Easier photo uploads (2026-10-17) {#v3-1}

Photos for news, pages, banners, events, institutions and members are uploaded directly in
the editing form. A preview and upload progress appear immediately; the photo is attached
when **Save** is pressed. Profile and duty-period photo uploads have also been updated.

Website photos are sharper and sized for the screen to help pages load faster.

Guide: [Uploading photos](/svetaine/failai#formu-nuotraukos)

- 🔧 **Resources** – adding or removing a photo also updates the photo in the resource list. Guide: [Resources](/rezervacijos/istekliai#kaip-tai-veikia)

## v3.0 — The refreshed My VU SR (2026-10-02) {#v3-0}

[Download the v3.0 PDF](/vusa-lt-v3-atnaujinimai.pdf) (Lithuanian, with annotated screenshots).
The PDF covers only the initial v3.0 release.

Links after *Guide* open the detailed description in this guide (in Lithuanian); links after
*My VU SR* (marked ↗) open that page of the platform in a new tab.

### 1. New design {#v3-0-dizainas}

My VU SR now looks like vusa.lt: the same VU SR colours and fonts, fewer boxes, clearer headings and
more room for content.

Home now starts with your tasks, upcoming meetings and an unfinished reservation. The ViSAK overview
covers your institutions, and unit metrics and the timeline have moved to a separate section.

The platform is built for phones and tablets too: larger buttons, every action reachable without a
mouse, and a bottom bar with Home, tasks, **Actions**, notifications and the menu.

Almost every form has been redone. The top bar shows whether your changes are saved, and settings
are grouped at the side.

✨ Creating a member, duty or another work record in a standalone form opens its record page.
Content and catalog entries open in their editor; inline creation keeps the current task open.
Guide: [After creation](/pagrindai/platforma#po-sukurimo).

Guide: [Home](/mano/), [ViSAK](/visak/apzvalga), [Units](/visak/padaliniai), [forms](/pagrindai/platforma#formu-antrastes) · My VU SR: [Home](app:/mano), [ViSAK](app:/mano/dashboard/atstovavimas), [Units](app:/mano/dashboard/atstovavimas/padaliniai)

### 2. Workspaces instead of the sidebar {#v3-0-darbo-sritys}

The sidebar has been replaced by workspaces: My, ViSAK, Reservations, Website, Organization and
System. Once you pick a workspace at the top, only the sections available to you are shown.

Search and the command palette (**Ctrl K**) open any page or record, and some quick actions are
available from **Actions**, marked with a click-pointer icon.

Guide: [Workspaces and search](/pagrindai/platforma)

### 3. A reservation cart {#v3-0-rezervacijos}

Reservations now work like a cart (just like an online shop 😅).

Items are picked in the resource list, which shows items from every unit and how many are free at
your chosen time, with your cart at the bottom. The chosen items then move to the reservation form.

An unfinished reservation is saved automatically and available on another device, but it does not
hold any items.

Guide: [Cart and submission](/rezervacijos/rezervacijos), [resources](/rezervacijos/istekliai) · My VU SR: [Resources](app:/mano/resources), [new reservation](app:/mano/reservations/create)

### 4. Notification settings {#v3-0-pranesimai}

For each notification type, you can choose how to receive it: an immediate email, a digest or no
email. Device (push) notifications are switched on separately, and notifications can be muted for a
while. A notification you have read no longer goes into an unsent digest.

Notification emails and digests open with a first-name greeting and use compact neutral action buttons. The footer briefly introduces Mano VU SA and links to the platform.

Registration emails (e.g. member registrations) to duty inboxes are always sent.

Guide: [Notifications](/pagrindai/pranesimai), [bell](/mano/pranesimai#kaip-tai-veikia) · My VU SR: [Notification settings](app:/mano/profile/notifications)

### Student representatives {#v3-0-atstovams}

#### Replying to a coordinator's request {#v3-0-atsakymas}

Coordinators can now ask representatives whether a meeting took place, or ask them to complete
agendas and decisions. The question arrives by email, and the answer is picked right in it.

The page that opens, without signing in to My VU SR, takes the meeting dates, times and formats.
Emails and requests use short, clear wording. Times are suggested every 15 minutes; exact times
can still be typed. The agenda item editor uses the same intervals.

Guide: [Replying to a request](/visak/institucijos#atsakymas)

- ✨ **More information in ViSAK** — public institution and meeting information is now also shown in
  ViSAK. Access to meetings held during your duty remains after it ends.
  Guide: [Access](/pagrindai/teises#bazine-prieiga) · My VU SR: [Institutions](app:/mano/institutions)
- ⭐ **Upcoming duties** — if your duty starts in the future, you get access straight away to
  prepare. A member appears among current members and in public contacts only from the start date.
  Guide: [Membership](/pagrindai/padaliniai-ir-pareigybes#naryste-ir-autorizacija)
- ⭐ **Your access in one place** — the account menu lists current, upcoming and ended duties, roles,
  available sections and the history of duty changes. Guide: [Account and access](/mano/paskyra-ir-prieiga#prieiga) ·
  My VU SR: [My roles and duties](app:/mano/profile/roles)
- ⭐ **"Not displayed" agenda items** — an individual item of a public meeting can be hidden from the
  public. Visibility is chosen in the item editor. Visitors see only the item's position, a "Not
  displayed" label and, if given, an additional title. Guide: [Item visibility](/visak/darbotvarkes-klausimai#matomumas)
- ✨ **"No meeting held" covers the past too** — the period now starts at the beginning of your term or
  the day after the last meeting, so one report can say there were no meetings until today and, if
  needed, for a few weeks ahead. On the home page this action sits next to "Record a meeting".
  Guide: [No meeting held](/visak/institucijos#posedzio-nebuvo) · My VU SR: [Home](app:/mano)
- 🔧 **Type documents open for their audience** — type documents listed on duty, institution and home
  pages (e.g. templates shared by every committee) can now be opened by everyone whose duty or
  institution is of that type, not only by administrators. Guide: [Record files](/visak/failai#teises)

### Student representative coordinators {#v3-0-koordinatoriams}

#### Ask representatives {#v3-0-paklausti}

Representatives can now be asked straight from the platform, without waiting for the automatic
reminder or emailing each one. A request starts from **Needs attention** in **ViSAK → Units**, from
**Actions** or from the institution's **⋯** menu.

There are two questions — **Did a meeting take place?** or **Complete meeting records** — sent
either to institutions or to specific representatives. Before sending, every recipient, their period
and email preferences are shown; recipients can be unticked and a note added.

In request emails and digests, each institution has its own reply actions.

Guide: [Ask representatives](/visak/institucijos#paklausti) · My VU SR: [ViSAK → Units](app:/mano/dashboard/atstovavimas/padaliniai)

#### Request history {#v3-0-uzklausu-istorija}

The institution's **Requests to representatives** section shows every sending: who asked, whom, for
which period, and what the representatives replied.

When an institution has several representatives and one of them answers, the others see what is
already recorded and can still add a meeting their colleague did not know about.

Guide: [Request history](/visak/institucijos#uzklausos), [several representatives](/visak/institucijos#keli-atstovai) · My VU SR: [Institutions](app:/mano/institutions)

- ✨ **Empty agenda item sections** — a missing description, student position, problem or goal no longer takes up its own section. One row of actions above the content holds “Change type” and dashed buttons (“Add a description”, “Link problem”…) that open that field or the linking sheet. The type is chosen in its own window instead of a button strip in the item's content. Guide: [Agenda items](/visak/darbotvarkes-klausimai).

#### Problems linked to agenda items {#v3-0-problemos}

A problem can now be linked to the agenda item where it was discussed.

The problem's **Discussed in meetings** section lists every such meeting, and the problem also gets
those meetings' institutions.

Guide: [Problems and meetings](/visak/problemos#posedziai) · My VU SR: [Problems](app:/mano/problems)

- ⭐ **Coordination follows responsibilities** — a coordination responsibility belongs to a duty and
  can cover a specific VU body, its type or a unit. It determines who coordinates and who is
  notified. A secretary is appointed separately for an institutional term.
  Guide: [Responsibilities](/pagrindai/atsakomybes), [secretaries](/visak/institucijos#sekretoriai)
- ✨ **Assignment period manager** — many members' duty dates can be adjusted in one timeline, with
  suggested corrections and a full-screen view. Unsaved changes are shown apart from saved dates.
  Guide: [Period manager](/visak/pareigybiu-laikotarpiai) · My VU SR: [Periods](app:/mano/dutiables/timeline)


### Communication coordinators {#v3-0-komunikacijai}

#### Both languages in one window {#v3-0-kalbos}

The Lithuanian and English versions of a page or news item can now be edited side by side.

Each version is saved and published separately, and a colleague's newer version is never
overwritten without a warning.

Guide: [Language versions](/svetaine/puslapiai#kalbu-versijos) · My VU SR: [Pages](app:/mano/pages), [news](app:/mano/news)

#### Recovering unsaved changes {#v3-0-atkurimas}

While editing, a private copy is kept on the device and on the server. When you come back, it can
be restored with **Restore copy**.

The saved page does not change until **Save** is pressed.

Guide: [Recovery](/svetaine/puslapiai#atkurimas)

- ✨ **Content search** — pages and news can be found by their body text, with a matching excerpt.
  Guide: [Search](/pagrindai/platforma#paieska-ir-pasirinkimas)
- ✨ **Event type from the title** — when you create an event, its type is picked from the title until you choose one yourself.
  Guide: [Calendar](/svetaine/kalendorius)

### Resource administrators {#v3-0-istekliams}

The main change is the [reservation cart](#v3-0-rezervacijos): representatives submit reservations
that have already been checked, and unfinished ones do not hold items.

### Other changes and fixes {#v3-0-kiti}

- 🔧 **Closing an institution** — marking an institution inactive ends its members’ current duty assignments and the ex officio seats derived from them, preserving their history. Its public contact page becomes unavailable. The form explains the consequences and lists assignment periods that must be resolved first. Guide: [Institution status](/visak/institucijos#aktyvumas) · My VU SR: [Institutions](app:/mano/institutions)

- ✨ **Coordinator actions** — the actions window offers "Manage information", leading to every section
  you can open, instead of "New news article". News is created from the news list.
  My VU SR: [All sections](app:/mano/administration)

- ✨ **Quieter comments** — the section at the bottom of a record is now called **Comments** and no
  longer carries explanatory text. The comment box is shown straight away, so the page no longer
  jumps when you start typing. You can also mention a colleague with the **Mention** button.
  Guide: [Comments](/visak/komentarai#komentaro-ir-atsakymo-rasymas)
- ✨ **Guide PDF** — technical information and test references appear only in the guide website.
  Guide: [The guide and PDF](/ivadas#kaip-skaityti-puslapius)
- ✨ **Search understands word forms** — Lithuanian and English search finds other forms of a word,
  shows records with the word in their name first and highlights the match where it is already shown —
  a content excerpt replaces the short description, so news and document lists no longer shift while
  typing. In the palette every result names its type, and people and duties are no longer pushed down by
  news that mention them. Long filter option lists can be searched.
  Guide: [Search](/pagrindai/platforma#paieska-ir-pasirinkimas)
- ⭐ **Every SharePoint archive file in Documents** — the platform reads the SharePoint archive itself
  every 15 minutes, and new files arrive as "Waiting". Document managers see all their files and other
  units' published documents in one list or by SharePoint folder, and change a file's status
  ("Published" or "Hidden") by clicking it in the row — for several files at once too. Missing
  SharePoint metadata (unit, type, date, language) is flagged. Only document files (PDF, Word, Excel
  and similar) are included, and files that were already in the archive but never imported start out
  as "Hidden". Files deleted in SharePoint are marked "Removed from SharePoint" and can be deleted. The
  familiar "Pick from SharePoint" window stays in Documents and on meetings: a picked file is published
  (and, on a meeting, linked). A waiting meeting file can be published and linked straight from the
  meeting. While a public link is being created the list refreshes itself, and a failed one offers a
  retry. Guide: [Documents](/visak/dokumentai#laukia) · My VU SR: [Documents](app:/mano/documents)
- ⭐ **Recommended documents** — settings let you choose documents to show for certain search
  phrases (e.g. "stipendijos"). Recommendations appear above other results without duplicates.
  Guide: [Document settings](/sistema/nustatymai#dokumentai) · My VU SR: [Document settings](app:/mano/settings/documents)
- 🔧 **Duty names follow gender** — next to a specific member, in search and in public contacts, a
  duty name follows the member's pronouns or name (e.g. "koordinatorė"), unless the period is set to
  use the original name. Guide: [Duties](/organizacija/pareigybes#lytis-ir-dublikatai)
- ✨ **Contacts in one list** — an institution's contacts show every member in one list, with the
  duty on each person's card. When a duty's contacts are ordered by unit, the unit appears next to
  the duty (e.g. "Member of Parliament · VU SA MIF"); when ordered by study programme, the programme
  does. A unit's contacts list coordinators first, then curators, and a unit's card in the contacts
  category opens its coordinators. Guide: [Duties](/organizacija/pareigybes#kurimas)
- ✨ **Public contacts order** — the public contacts search lists VU SR and its bodies first (e.g. the
  Council, Parliament, Audit Commission), then the main institutions of units and PKP. When searching,
  these rise above similarly matching results. On phones, the quick categories sit under "Filters".
- ⭐ **Adding former members** — when assigning a duty you can pick a term: the current one starts
  today, an earlier one covers the whole term. Someone not yet in the system can be created on the
  spot, without an email. Extra settings are folded away, the study programme shows only for curators,
  and “Public description” is now “Additional description”.
  Guide: [Assigning a member](/organizacija/pareigybes#priskirti-nari) · My VU SR: [Duties](app:/mano/duties)
- ⭐ **Duty history** — a duty can be marked inactive, keep its former names and be linked to the duty
  whose work it took over (e.g. when one duty is split in two). Creating a duty also warns about similar
  inactive or formerly named duties, and similar duties elsewhere in the same unit are listed with a link.
  Guide: [Duty history](/organizacija/pareigybes#istorija)
- ✨ **Current and former members** — the member list can be narrowed to current or former members and
  shows the years and institutions each member served in, and pickers show current members first. Guide: [Member list](/organizacija/nariai#sarasas) ·
  My VU SR: [Members](app:/mano/users)
- 🔧 **The last day in office counts** — a member keeps membership and access until the end of their
  last day. The handover wizard updates one duty at a time, and creating a member record does not
  send an invitation. Guide: [Period dates](/organizacija/pareigybes#laikotarpio-datos),
  [handover](/organizacija/pareigybiu-atnaujinimas)
- 🔧 **Revoked permissions apply at once** — after a permission is removed from a role, a role from a
  duty, or a role is deleted, members lose that access from the next page they open, not an hour later.
  Guide: [How the system decides](/pagrindai/teises#kaip-sistema-nusprendzia) · My VU SR: [Roles](app:/mano/roles)
- ✨ **What vusa.lt shows** — on a meeting, **What is shown?** next to **Visibility** explains what of
  the meeting is published and what never is. The **Files** tab reminds you that files are never
  shown on vusa.lt. Guide: [File visibility](/visak/failai#viesumas)
- ⭐ **Revoke a file link** — an opened file is reachable through a link that needs no sign-in. Such a
  link can now be revoked, and it stops working at once. Guide: [Revoking a link](/visak/failai#atsaukti)
- 🔧 **A more reliable file usage check** — **Tikrinti** finds a file in every content block, image
  field, link and unsaved draft, even when its name has spaces or Lithuanian letters. The list shows
  where the file is used. Guide: [Usage check](/svetaine/failai#naudojimo-patikra) ·
  My VU SR: [Files](app:/mano/files)
- ✨ **Separate types and simpler documents** — institution and duty types are managed separately,
  and a new type can be created directly from its list. Every member can browse the document
  archive. Guide: [Types](/sistema/tipai), [documents](/visak/dokumentai) ·
  My VU SR: [Documents](app:/mano/documents)
- ✨ **Smaller member photos** — institution, duty, meeting and reservation cards leave more room for
  names and details. Guide: [Institution page](/visak/institucijos#ka-rodo-institucijos-puslapis)
- ✨ **A more compact bell** — the bell list and notification page show actions as icons beside the
  read mark. My VU SR: [Notifications](app:/mano/notifications)
- ✨ **Feature hints on phones** — on a phone or tablet, a new feature's hint opens by itself once its
  button appears on screen. Scrolling past closes it, and the corner notch reopens it.
- ⭐ **System maintenance and support** — super administrators can run maintenance actions from the
  system status page, and support requests let members report a problem and follow its resolution.
  Guide: [System status](/sistema/sistemos-busena), [support](/sistema/pagalbos-uzklausos) ·
  My VU SR: [New support request](app:/mano/my-support-requests/create)
- ⭐ **Goals (pilot)** — only enabled units can link goals to completed actions, problems and agenda
  items and publish selected goals on their website. Guide: [Goals](/visak/tikslai)
