# Platform updates: v3

## v3.0 — The refreshed My VU SR (2026-10-02) {#v3-0}

The new version of My VU SR, prepared in September and early October. The main changes come first,
then what changes for your role, with smaller fixes at the end. The guide already describes this
version; what already runs on vusa.lt is listed in v2. The date shows when this summary was checked,
not when the version went live on vusa.lt. Guide links below open Lithuanian instructions.

[Download the PDF](/vusa-lt-v3-atnaujinimai.pdf) (Lithuanian, with annotated screenshots).

### 1. New design {#v3-0-dizainas}

My VU SR now looks like vusa.lt: the same VU SR colours and fonts, fewer boxes, clearer headings and
more room for content. Home starts with your tasks, upcoming meetings and an unfinished reservation;
the ViSAK overview covers your institutions, and unit metrics and the timeline have a separate
section. [Home](/mano/), [ViSAK](/visak/apzvalga), [Units](/visak/padaliniai)

Almost every form has been redone: the top bar shows whether your changes are saved, the name you
type appears in the main heading straight away, and settings are grouped at the side. Phones and
tablets are first-class: larger buttons, nothing hidden behind hover, and a bottom bar with Home,
tasks, **+ Create**, notifications and the menu. [Guide](/pagrindai/platforma#formu-antrastes)

### 2. Workspaces instead of the sidebar {#v3-0-darbo-sritys}

The sidebar is gone. Pick a workspace at the top — My, ViSAK, Reservations, Website, Organization or
System — and see only the sections available to you. Search and the command palette (**Ctrl K**)
take you to any page or record, and **+ Create** is the one place for everything you can create.
[Guide](/pagrindai/platforma)

### 3. A reservation cart {#v3-0-rezervacijos}

An unfinished reservation is saved automatically and persists across devices without holding
items. **Submit** checks availability again; deleted unfinished reservations do not go to trash.
Pick items in the resource list, which shows how many are free at your chosen time, with your cart
always at the bottom. [Cart and submission](/rezervacijos/rezervacijos), [resources](/rezervacijos/istekliai)

### 4. Notification settings {#v3-0-pranesimai}

For each notification type, choose how to receive it: an immediate email, a digest or no email.
Device (push) notifications are switched on separately, and notifications can be muted for a while.
Read notifications leave unsent digests; registration emails to duty inboxes remain mandatory.
[Notifications](/pagrindai/pranesimai), [bell](/mano/pranesimai#kaip-tai-veikia)

### Student representatives {#v3-0-atstovams}

#### Replying to a coordinator's request {#v3-0-atsakymas}

A coordinator can ask whether a meeting took place or ask you to complete agendas and decisions. Pick
an answer in the email and, without signing in, enter meeting dates, times and formats.
[Guide](/visak/institucijos#atsakymas)

- ✨ **More information without an extra role** — public institution and meeting information is available without an additional role. Access to meetings held during your assignment remains after it ends. [Access](/pagrindai/teises#bazine-prieiga)
- ⭐ **Upcoming duties** — if your duty starts in the future, you get its roles' access straight away to prepare. You appear among current members and in public contacts only from the start date. [Guide](/pagrindai/padaliniai-ir-pareigybes#naryste-ir-autorizacija)
- ⭐ **Inspect your own access** — the account menu lists current, upcoming and ended duties, roles, available sections and dated duty changes. [Guide](/mano/paskyra-ir-prieiga#prieiga)
- ✨ **One meeting-completion rule** — meeting status, the automatic task and agenda filters agree on completion, including the exceptions for informational and deferred items, breaks and VU SR bodies. [Guide](/visak/darbotvarkes-klausimai#uzpildyta)
- ⭐ **Internal-only agenda items** — keep an individual item of a public meeting for internal work. Visitors see its number, a visibility label and an optional public title. [Guide](/visak/darbotvarkes-klausimai#matomumas)

### Student representative coordinators {#v3-0-koordinatoriams}

#### Ask representatives {#v3-0-paklausti}

No more waiting for the automatic reminder or emailing each representative. In **ViSAK → Units**,
press **Ask representatives** beside **Needs attention**; the same action is in **+ Create** and the
institution's **⋯** menu. Choose **Did a meeting take place?** or **Complete meeting records**,
then pick either institutions or specific representatives. Institutions and people there is nothing
to ask about are shown disabled, with the reason. Before sending, review every recipient (including
yourself if you are a representative), their period and email preferences, untick anyone you do not
want to ask, and add a note. [Guide](/visak/institucijos#paklausti)

#### Request history {#v3-0-uzklausu-istorija}

The institution's **Requests to representatives** section shows every sending: who asked, whom, for
which period, and what the representatives replied. When an institution has several representatives
and one of them answers, the others see who answered and what is already recorded, and can still add
a meeting their colleague did not know about. [Guide](/visak/institucijos#uzklausos)

#### Problems linked to agenda items {#v3-0-problemos}

Link a problem to the agenda item where representatives raised it. The problem's **Discussed in
meetings** section lists every meeting where it came up, and the problem also gets those meetings'
institutions. [Guide](/visak/problemos#posedziai)

- ⭐ **Coordination follows responsibilities** — a responsibility stays with a duty, can cover a VU body, its type or a unit, and determines coordinators and notification recipients. A secretary is appointed separately for an institutional term. [Responsibilities](/pagrindai/atsakomybes), [secretaries](/visak/institucijos#sekretoriai)
- ✨ **Assignment period manager** — adjust many assignments in one timeline, review suggested corrections and use full screen. [Guide](/visak/pareigybiu-laikotarpiai)

### Communication coordinators {#v3-0-komunikacijai}

#### Both languages in one window {#v3-0-kalbos}

Edit the Lithuanian and English versions of a page or news item in one window. Save and publish each
separately; a colleague's newer version is never silently overwritten. [Guide](/svetaine/puslapiai#kalbu-versijos)

#### Recovering unsaved changes {#v3-0-atkurimas}

While you edit, a private copy is kept on the device and the server. Come back and choose **Restore
copy**; the saved page does not change until you press **Save**. [Guide](/svetaine/puslapiai#atkurimas)

- ✨ **Content search** — find pages and news by their body text, with a matching excerpt. [Guide](/pagrindai/platforma#paieska-ir-pasirinkimas)

### Resource administrators {#v3-0-istekliams}

The main change is the [reservation cart](#v3-0-rezervacijos): representatives submit reservations that have already been checked, and unfinished ones do not hold items.

### Other changes and fixes {#v3-0-kiti}

- 🔧 **Consistent Lithuanian duty names** — generic headings alternate gender endings again while preserving their typography and using a solid brand underline; the form heading updates as you type. Names attached to a member follow their pronouns or name across profiles, search, timelines and public contacts, respecting each assignment's original-name setting. Role choices in **+ Create** also follow your profile's pronouns or name. [Guide](/organizacija/pareigybes#lytis-ir-dublikatai)
- ✨ **Search and clearer filters** — Lithuanian and English search recognises word forms, prioritises each record's own name or title and highlights matches within the heading, including public document search, which also matches word prefixes. Smaller excerpts strip HTML and avoid repeating the name or title. Facet counts keep the context of other filters, and longer option lists support option search. [Guide](/pagrindai/platforma#paieska-ir-pasirinkimas)
- ⭐ **Recommended documents** — choose documents, trigger phrases and their order in settings. Recommendations appear separately without duplicates and respect filters; important document types rank higher among equally relevant results. The default list is newest first, the sorting control reflects that order, and recommendations remain above it. [Guide](/sistema/nustatymai#dokumentai)
- ✨ **Compact navigation selectors** — the workspace button matches the search field height again, and the website's unit selector takes less space. Touch screens retain larger tap targets. [Guide](/pagrindai/platforma#mygtukai-ir-klaviatura)
- ✨ **Smaller member photos** — compact avatars in institution, duty, meeting and reservation cards leave more room for names and details. [Guide](/visak/institucijos#ka-rodo-institucijos-puslapis)
- ✨ **Agenda item visibility** — choose visibility in the item editor and expand the access explanation when needed. Without a public title, visitors see "Not displayed". [Guide](/visak/darbotvarkes-klausimai#matomumas)
- 🔧 **The last day in office counts** — membership and access remain active until that day ends. The handover wizard updates one duty at a time; creating a member record does not send an invitation. [Dates](/organizacija/pareigybes#laikotarpio-datos), [handover](/organizacija/pareigybiu-atnaujinimas)
- ✨ **A more compact bell** — the bell list and notification page use action icons beside the read checkmark to save space. [Guide](/mano/pranesimai#kaip-tai-veikia)
- ✨ **Request emails and digests** — emails and digests provide separate actions for each institution. [Guide](/visak/institucijos#paklausti)
- ✨ **What vusa.lt shows** — on a meeting, **What is shown?** next to **Visibility** explains what of the meeting is published and what never is. The **Files** tab reminds you that files are never shown on vusa.lt. [Guide](/visak/failai#viesumas)
- ⭐ **Revoke a file link** — an opened file is reachable through a link that needs no sign-in; you can now revoke it, and the old link stops working at once. [Guide](/visak/failai#atsaukti)
- ✨ **Period manager drafts** — unsaved changes remain distinct from saved dates. [Guide](/visak/pareigybiu-laikotarpiai)
- ✨ **Separate types and simpler documents** — manage institution and duty types independently and create a new type directly from its list. Every member can browse the document archive; document managers control SharePoint synchronization and removal. [Types](/sistema/tipai), [documents](/visak/dokumentai)
- 🔧 **A more reliable file usage check** — **Tikrinti** now finds a file in every content block, image field, link and unsaved draft, even when its name has spaces or Lithuanian letters. The result is always fresh, and the list shows where the file is used with a link to each record. [Guide](/svetaine/failai#naudojimo-patikra)
- ⭐ **System maintenance from the application** — super administrators can run maintenance actions from System status; support requests let members report problems and follow their resolution. [System status](/sistema/sistemos-busena), [support](/sistema/pagalbos-uzklausos)
- ⭐ **Goals remain a pilot** — enabled units can connect goals, completed actions, problems and agenda items and publish selected goals on their website. [Guide](/visak/tikslai)
- ✨ **Feature hints on phones** — on a phone or tablet without a mouse, a new feature's hint opens by itself once its button settles on screen. Scrolling past closes it, and the corner notch reopens it.
