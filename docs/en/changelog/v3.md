# Platform updates: v3

## 2026-10-31 — Questions about institution activity

- ✨ Coordinators can now send **Did a meeting take place?** to institutions with no recorded meetings or activity reports, without waiting for the periodicity deadline. Guide: [Asking representatives](/visak/institucijos#paklausti) · My VU SR: [Institutions](app:/mano/institutions)
- ✨ For these institutions, the automatic question now counts from the day the representative took up the duty, so a new term no longer restarts the wait for continuing representatives. Guide: [Tasks](/mano/uzduotys)
- 🔧 The reason shown for **Complete meeting records** now says there is nothing to complete, and only suggests **Did a meeting take place?** when that question can be sent.

## 2026-10-30 — Ex officio review

Ex officio terms are now compared with the source duty held at the same time, not with today's status.

- ⭐ **Review ex officio** – a duty's page can now review its whole history: link terms to their source, add missing ones, align dates and delete duplicates. Dates and consequences are shown before anything is applied. Guide: [Checking the whole history](/visak/pareigybiu-laikotarpiai#ex-officio-tikrinimas) · My VU SR: [Duties](app:/mano/duties).
- 🔧 The suspect ex officio warning no longer flags people who did not hold a source duty at that time.
- 🔧 The term manager now lists every valid source, including ones that place the term on a unit's quota.
- 🔧 Activity questions no longer go to representatives whose term has not started.
- ✨ **Ex officio duties under the main duty** – a person's page now shows these duties under the duty that gives them, labelled, and no longer offers to end them separately. Guide: [How ex officio terms follow the main duty](/visak/pareigybiu-laikotarpiai#ex-officio-sekimas).
- ✨ Ex officio terms now update the moment you save, so the warning about losing your own access counts them too. When you change a source in the term manager, their changes appear in the preview.
- ✨ The system status page shows a nightly check of ex officio terms.
- 🔧 When an institution reopens, ex officio duties resume from that day.
- 🔧 Merging duties or terms keeps closure history, and existing holders get the ex officio duties they are due.
- 🔧 A person's form can no longer end an ex officio duty by accident: it explains that the duty follows its source.

## 2026-10-28 — More consistent forms and collections, representation settings and fixes

- 🔧 Deleting a tag also updates the result count. The deletion can be undone from the notification. Guide: [Tags](/svetaine/zymos#redagavimas-ir-salinimas).
- ✨ **Consistent row actions** – each row of a collection table shows one action with a label (e.g. “Edit” or a task's “Agenda”), the other open and edit actions as icons, and the rest, such as deleting, in the ⋯ menu. The actions column stays visible while scrolling a wide table. Guide: [Row actions](/pagrindai/platforma#eilutes-veiksmai).
- 🔧 Most collections can be sorted by clicking a column heading, e.g. problems by date or status, meetings by date, resources by name. Names sort in the selected language, and a saved link with a sort opens in that order straight away.
- ✨ Administrators can confirm independent assignments or explicitly link them to a source, with a confirmation explaining date and deletion consequences. Guide: [Term manager](/visak/pareigybiu-laikotarpiai).
- 🔧 Merging source terms preserves their linked assignments and independent details. Review warnings no longer repeat for each possible source. Guide: [Term manager](/visak/pareigybiu-laikotarpiai).
- ✨ **More consistent My VU SR forms** – required fields are marked with an asterisk and one note at the top of the form; field labels no longer carry “(optional)” or a language tag, as the language is switched for the whole form. Unit, category or status are picked from a browser list or a button group, and settings saved with the form are ticked with a checkbox. Guide: [Languages and translations](/pagrindai/platforma#vertimai).
- 🔧 Saving a support request shows a readable message instead of technical text. The navigation page heading matches other lists, and navigation link forms show their titles correctly. My VU SR: [Navigation](app:/mano/navigation).
- 🔧 Choosing English now shows translated institution and resource names, types and categories in collections and search, and English institution names in meeting, agenda item and document collections. Guide: [Institutions](/visak/institucijos#kaip-tai-veikia).
- ✨ The “Institution types without meetings” setting is gone: representation metrics and Home reminders count only institutions of the ticked bodies.
- ✨ The student representative body root type no longer has a default – with no type selected, public contacts show no student representative cards. Guide: [Settings](/sistema/nustatymai#atstovavimas).

## 2026-10-27 — Duty history

A duty's history is now shown on a dated timeline: renames, merges and splits appear in one place, and predecessors are linked with the date the new duty took over the work. Duties that were merged and later split again can be linked again without creating new records.

- ⭐ **“It was only a rename”** – if a rename was once recorded as a new duty, it can be folded into the current one: its members move over and the old name is kept as a former name. Guide: [Duty history](/organizacija/pareigybes#istorija) · My VU SR: [Duties](app:/mano/duties).
- ⭐ **“With history” filter** – the duty list can show only duties that were renamed or linked to other duties.
- ✨ **A calmer duty form** – former names sit in a collapsible list under the name, and rarer parts (former names, other units’ representatives) are added with dashed buttons at the bottom of the form.
- ✨ **“Operating” and “Not operating”** – duty and institution statuses were renamed from “Active” and “Inactive” so they are not confused with meeting activity.
- 🔧 **Editing from the duty list** – duties you can edit show **Edit** again in the list and the preview. My VU SR: [Duties](app:/mano/duties).

## 2026-10-27 — Forms, START FM player and other fixes

- 🔧 Mailing lists using **Duty mailbox** now reach people who hold a seat because of another duty (e.g. unit chairs on the Council) at that duty's mailbox, such as `pirmininkas@ff.vusa.lt`, instead of their personal email. The recipients list marks anyone who still gets their personal email because the duty has no mailbox. Guide: [Which address](/organizacija/konferencijos#adresas) · My VU SR: [Mailing lists](app:/mano/mailingLists).
- ✨ The public website and My VU SR use the same compact radio player with pause, volume and mute controls. Choosing “Listen to START FM” starts the stream immediately.
- 🔧 On phones, the player sits above navigation or form Save controls. Connection errors offer a retry action.
- ✨ Language switching appears directly in the account menu. “My activity requests” no longer shows an introductory popover.
- 🔧 Checkbox ticks no longer briefly darken when selected. The fix covers the shared checkbox and larger controls in the accessibility menu.
- ✨ Registration forms and the field editor use native dropdowns. Placeholder text appears in a muted colour until an answer is selected.
- 🔧 The field editing sheet now has padding around its contents.
- ✨ Public registration pages show the existing “You can edit this page” notification to users with permission to update the form, linking directly to its editor.
- ✨ Field errors appear below their questions without being repeated above the form. A failed submission focuses the first invalid field; general errors and errors for hidden answers remain visible at the top.
- 🔧 Registration checkboxes appear beside their labels. Clicking the label also toggles the checkbox.
- 🔧 Representative requests opened from **ViSAK → Units** inherit the selected units and default to VU bodies. The filters remain editable in the dialog.
- ✨ Select matching institutions together, automatically loading more results up to 100 institutions, or clear the selection. Bulk selection is also available in the recipient review. Unit and governance scope filters fit on one line, so the institution list is visible straight away. Guide: [Asking representatives](/visak/institucijos#paklausti)
- 🔧 **Old periodicity tasks** – a “Report activity” task left over from the previous term, for an institution with no meetings yet whose new representatives do not owe a report yet, or for an institution that is no longer operating, is now marked **Cancelled**. Nobody is notified and representative metrics leave it out. “Did a meeting take place?” also reaches people who receive these emails as a digest. Guide: [Cancelled tasks](/mano/uzduotys#atsauktos)
- ✨ Edit sheets show when the record was created and last updated below the fields. Sheets and full forms share the same date and time display. Guide: [Record dates in forms](/pagrindai/platforma#formu-datos)
- 🔧 The tag editing form opens without an error, and the description editor also appears for tags without a description. Guide: [Tags](/svetaine/zymos#naujos-zymos-kurimas) · My VU SR: [Tags](app:/mano/tags)
- 🔧 Restoring or permanently deleting records refreshes the trash list automatically. The fix covers institutions, meetings, members, news, pages, calendar events and quick links.
- 🔧 **Duty title endings** – Lithuanian deputy titles now use the correct “Pavaduotojas / Pavaduotoja” forms, with improved gender inference from first names. Duplicate warnings also recognise the feminine plural of this duty. Guide: [Endings and duplicates](/organizacija/pareigybes#lytis-ir-dublikatai)
- 🔧 **Esc in forms** – pressing **Esc** in a field only leaves the field; the form page no longer closes. Guide: [Keys in forms](/pagrindai/platforma#formu-klavisai)
- 🔧 **News archive links** – when showing news from all units, each link opens the article on its publishing unit’s site. Guide: [News](/svetaine/naujienos#kaip-tai-veikia)
- ✨ **Tenant settings** – search for a primary institution and see it in the tenant list. Field hints explain where values are used; only institutions belonging to the same tenant can be selected. Guide: [Tenants](/organizacija/padaliniai#kurimas) · My VU SR: [Tenants](app:/mano/tenants)
- 🔧 **Unit photos** – the public unit contacts category shows uploaded institution photos again, falling back to the logo when no photo is available. Guide: [Institutions](/visak/institucijos#rusis-ir-tipai)
- ✨ **Waiting SharePoint files on a meeting** – no longer look like already linked documents: they open from the **Waiting in SharePoint** button, where you can search and pick several files at once. Guide: [Changing a meeting](/visak/posedziai#keitimas)

## 2026-10-20 — Unit status and representative metrics

The four status counts sit in one row at the top, with the attention list and the status chart side by side below. Below the meeting timeline, term metrics appear beside current representative activity.

Guide: [Units](/visak/padaliniai#atstovavimo-rodikliai) · My VU SR: [Units](app:/mano/dashboard/atstovavimas/padaliniai)

### Other changes and fixes

- ✨ **Main vote is optional** – an agenda item can hold several related votes with no main one. Its status then follows all the votes, and shows “Mixed results” when they differ. The public meeting page lists each vote. Guide: [Votes](/visak/darbotvarkes-klausimai#balsavimai).
- ✨ **Former members fill in their meetings** – anyone who served on the institution on the meeting day can fill in its agenda items and votes, even after their duty has ended. Their changes show in the meeting history. Guide: [Who can do what](/visak/posedziai#teises).
- ⭐ **Term comparison** – the current term so far is compared with the entire previous term. Administrators can select another completed term in settings. Guide: [Representation settings](/sistema/nustatymai#atstovavimas).
- ✨ **Clearer overview** – three metrics show both date ranges and sample counts, alongside four activity counters and three recently active representatives. Term years are shortened, with exact dates and calculation rules in expandable details. Chart details float above the page content; status colors are solid and the term-end label sits above the area. The attention preview shows four institutions, and activity bars adapt their grouping to the selected range and chart width. Guide: [Units](/visak/padaliniai).
- ⭐ **“Withdrawn” agenda item type** – an item struck from the agenda or withdrawn no longer has to be marked “Deferred”. It needs no vote. Guide: [Item type](/visak/darbotvarkes-klausimai#tipai).
- ✨ **Clearer type choice** – “Informational” is renamed “Discussion” and covers both presentations and discussions without a vote. “Voting” also covers decisions by consensus, and the “Item type” window's question follows whether the meeting has happened and whether it is decided by email. Guide: [Item type](/visak/darbotvarkes-klausimai#tipai).
- ✨ **Metrics count VU bodies** – unit counts, the attention list, status changes and representative metrics now cover only VU bodies and their representatives by default. VU SA bodies still appear on the meeting timeline, and settings can change which bodies count. Guide: [Which institutions count](/visak/padaliniai#skaiciuojamos-institucijos) · My VU SR: [Representation settings](app:/mano/settings/atstovavimas)
- ✨ **Subtypes inherit responsibilities** – a responsibility given to a duty type also reaches the duties of its subtypes, e.g. *Chair* duties receive what the *Coordinator* type is given. The type page marks inherited responsibilities. Guide: [Duty type responsibilities](/sistema/tipai#pareigybes-tipo-atsakomybes)
- ✨ **Safer type grants** – a duty type can no longer be given a responsibility with “Everywhere” or “Delete permanently” permissions; such a responsibility is given to duties one by one. Guide: [Granting a responsibility](/sistema/roles-ir-leidimai#atsakomybes-suteikimas)
- ✨ **Types shown as a tree** – when picking an institution or duty type, subtypes appear under their parent and search shows the full path. Guide: [Types and categories](/sistema/tipai#veiksmai)
- 🔧 **Consistent calculations** – missing historical days appear as gaps, check-in grace is counted from coverage ending, and tasks belong to their institution or meeting’s unit, excluding reservations. Agenda metrics now use item completion rules for each item type. Guide: [Status rules](/visak/apzvalga#busenos).

- 🔧 **Loading meetings on dashboards** – reloading the units dashboard several times in a row no longer fails with “Failed to load meetings”. The request limit now counts each user separately rather than everyone on the same network.

## 2026-10-19 — Responsibilities instead of roles

Roles became **responsibilities**: a responsibility says what a duty does and grants the permissions
that work needs. Every former role was kept with the same permissions, so nobody's access changed. A
responsibility can be given to a duty, a duty type or, rarely, a whole institution.

Guide: [Responsibilities](/pagrindai/atsakomybes), [Responsibilities and permissions](/sistema/roles-ir-leidimai) · My VU SR: [Responsibilities](app:/mano/responsibilities)

### Other changes and fixes

- ✨ **Duty type responsibilities** – grants and their scope are managed on the responsibility page; the type record shows their list and links. Assigning types requires delegation and access to the affected duty.
  Guide: [Duty types](/sistema/tipai#pareigybes-tipo-atsakomybes)

- ✨ **Permission grid** – all of a responsibility's permissions are seen and changed in one table grouped by workspace, with one “Save” and a summary of changes. Permissions for mailing lists, goals, study sets and event types now appear in it too. On wider screens, column headings stay visible while scrolling.
  Guide: [Setting permissions](/sistema/roles-ir-leidimai#veiksmai)
- ⭐ **Title and description** – a responsibility has a Lithuanian and English title and description.
  Guide: [Creating a responsibility](/sistema/roles-ir-leidimai#veiksmai)
- ✨ **Given to and held by** – the responsibility page shows the duties, types and institutions it is given to and who holds it now. The duty page also shows responsibilities it gets through its type or institution.
  Guide: [Giving a responsibility](/sistema/roles-ir-leidimai#veiksmai)
- ⭐ **System purposes** – settings say which responsibility counts as student representation, coordination and member registration, and which types appear in contacts as coordinators, curators and mentors.
  Guide: [System purposes](/sistema/nustatymai#sistemos-paskirtys) · My VU SR: [Settings](app:/mano/settings)
- ✨ **Member registrations** – registrations go to and are visible for duties holding the “Member registration” responsibility; form settings show where nobody receives them.
  Guide: [Form settings](/sistema/nustatymai#formos)
- ⭐ **Mailing lists by responsibility** – a mailing list can take its recipients by responsibility, e.g. every student representative coordinator.
  Guide: [Recipient sources](/organizacija/konferencijos)
- ✨ **Clearer permission grid** – the grid no longer offers actions that grant nothing (e.g. “Own” for tasks or users), rows follow the menu order, and each resource says what every member can do without a responsibility. “Delete” is now “Move to trash” and “Delete permanently”.
  Guide: [Setting permissions](/sistema/roles-ir-leidimai#veiksmai), [What every member can do](/pagrindai/teises#bazine-prieiga)
- ✨ **Agenda items follow the meeting** – whoever holds the right to edit meetings (e.g. student representatives) creates, edits and deletes the meeting's agenda items; no separate permissions are needed.
  Guide: [What every member can do](/pagrindai/teises#bazine-prieiga)
- ✨ **Comments editable for 15 minutes** – you can edit your comment for 15 minutes after writing it; deleting works any time.
  Guide: [What every member can do](/pagrindai/teises#bazine-prieiga)
- ✨ **Reservation deletion** – a participant can delete their reservation until none of its items is approved.
  Guide: [What every member can do](/pagrindai/teises#bazine-prieiga)
- ⭐ **Viewing settings** – the new “Settings · view” permission opens settings without the ability to change them.
  Guide: [Who can change settings](/sistema/nustatymai#autorizacija) · My VU SR: [Settings](app:/mano/settings)
- 🔧 **Safer granting** – a coordinator editing a duty can only give responsibilities they hold themselves; the duty form no longer changes responsibilities.
  Guide: [Who can do what](/sistema/roles-ir-leidimai#teises)

  category opens its coordinators. Guide: [Duties](/organizacija/pareigybes#kurimas)
- ✨ **Public contacts order** — the public contacts search lists VU SR and its bodies first (e.g. the
  Council, Parliament, Audit Commission), then the main institutions of units and PKP. When searching,
  these rise above similarly matching results. On phones, the quick categories sit under "Filters".
- ⭐ **Adding former members** — when assigning a duty you can pick a term: the current one starts
  today, an earlier one covers the whole term. Someone not yet in the system can be created on the
  spot, without an email. Extra settings are folded away, the study programme shows only for curators,
  and “Public description” is now “Additional description”.
  Guide: [Assigning a member](/organizacija/pareigybes#priskirti-nari) · My VU SR: [Duties](app:/mano/duties)
- ⭐ **Duty history** — a duty can be marked inactive, keep its former names and be linked to the duty
  whose work it took over (e.g. when one duty is split in two). Creating a duty also warns about similar
  inactive or formerly named duties, and similar duties elsewhere in the same unit are listed with a link.
  Guide: [Duty history](/organizacija/pareigybes#istorija)
- ✨ **Current and former members** — the member list can be narrowed to current or former members and
  shows the years and institutions each member served in, and pickers show current members first. Guide: [Member list](/organizacija/nariai#sarasas) ·
  My VU SR: [Members](app:/mano/users)
- 🔧 **The last day in office counts** — a member keeps membership and access until the end of their
  last day. The handover wizard updates one duty at a time, and creating a member record does not
  send an invitation. Guide: [Period dates](/organizacija/pareigybes#laikotarpio-datos),
  [handover](/organizacija/pareigybiu-atnaujinimas)
- 🔧 **Revoked permissions apply at once** — after a permission is removed from a role, a role from a
  duty, or a role is deleted, members lose that access from the next page they open, not an hour later.
  Guide: [How the system decides](/pagrindai/teises#kaip-sistema-nusprendzia) · My VU SR: [Roles](app:/mano/roles)
- ✨ **What vusa.lt shows** — on a meeting, **What is shown?** next to **Visibility** explains what of
  the meeting is published and what never is. The **Files** tab reminds you that files are never
  shown on vusa.lt. Guide: [File visibility](/visak/failai#viesumas)
- ⭐ **Revoke a file link** — an opened file is reachable through a link that needs no sign-in. Such a
  link can now be revoked, and it stops working at once. Guide: [Revoking a link](/visak/failai#atsaukti)
- 🔧 **A more reliable file usage check** — **Tikrinti** finds a file in every content block, image
  field, link and unsaved draft, even when its name has spaces or Lithuanian letters. The list shows
  where the file is used. Guide: [Usage check](/svetaine/failai#naudojimo-patikra) ·
  My VU SR: [Files](app:/mano/files)
- ✨ **Separate types and simpler documents** — institution and duty types are managed separately,
  and a new type can be created directly from its list. Every member can browse the document
  archive. Guide: [Types](/sistema/tipai), [documents](/visak/dokumentai) ·
  My VU SR: [Documents](app:/mano/documents)
- ✨ **Smaller member photos** — institution, duty, meeting and reservation cards leave more room for
  names and details. Guide: [Institution page](/visak/institucijos#ka-rodo-institucijos-puslapis)
- ✨ **A more compact bell** — the bell list and notification page show actions as icons beside the
  read mark. My VU SR: [Notifications](app:/mano/notifications)
- ✨ **Feature hints on phones** — on a phone or tablet, a new feature's hint opens by itself once its
  button appears on screen. Scrolling past closes it, and the corner notch reopens it.
- ⭐ **System maintenance and support** — super administrators can run maintenance actions from the
  system status page, and support requests let members report a problem and follow its resolution.
  Guide: [System status](/sistema/sistemos-busena), [support](/sistema/pagalbos-uzklausos) ·
  My VU SR: [New support request](app:/mano/my-support-requests/create)
- ⭐ **Goals (pilot)** — only enabled units can link goals to completed actions, problems and agenda
  items and publish selected goals on their website. Guide: [Goals](/visak/tikslai)
