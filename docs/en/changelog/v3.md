# Platform updates: v3

## v3.0 — The refreshed My VU SR (2026-10-02) {#v3-0}

The new version of My VU SR, prepared in September and early October, with its main changes in
one place. The guide already describes this version; what already runs on vusa.lt is listed in v2.
The date shows when this summary was checked, not when the version went live on vusa.lt.
Guide links below open Lithuanian instructions.

- ⭐ **Workspaces and one creation menu** — My, ViSAK, Reservations, Website, Organization, and System show the sections available to you. **+ Create**, the command palette, and mobile bars bring the same work within reach across devices. [Guide](/pagrindai/platforma)
- ✨ **Home starts with your work** — tasks, upcoming meetings, and unfinished reservations come first. The ViSAK overview covers your institutions; unit metrics and the timeline have a separate section. [Home](/mano/), [ViSAK](/visak/apzvalga), [Units](/visak/padaliniai)
- ⭐ **Inspect your own access** — the account menu lists current, upcoming, and ended duties, roles, available sections, and dated duty changes. [Guide](/mano/paskyra-ir-prieiga#prieiga)
- ✨ **Clearer baseline access** — public institution and meeting information is available without an additional role. Access to meetings held during your assignment remains after it ends; upcoming assignments allow preparation before public contact listings start. [Access](/pagrindai/teises#bazine-prieiga), [dates](/pagrindai/padaliniai-ir-pareigybes#datos)
- ⭐ **Coordination follows responsibilities** — a responsibility stays with a duty, can cover a VU body, its type, or a unit, and determines coordinators and notification recipients. A secretary is appointed separately for an institutional term. [Responsibilities](/pagrindai/atsakomybes), [secretaries](/visak/institucijos#sekretoriai)
- 🔧 **The last day in office counts** — membership and access remain active until that day ends. The handover wizard updates one duty at a time; creating a member record does not send an invitation. [Dates](/organizacija/pareigybes#laikotarpio-datos), [handover](/organizacija/pareigybiu-atnaujinimas)
- ✨ **Notifications follow your choices** — select immediate email, a digest, or no email for each type, with device notifications controlled separately. Muting preserves in-app records; read notifications leave unsent digests. Registration emails to duty inboxes remain mandatory. [Guide](/pagrindai/pranesimai)
- ⭐ **A reservation cart** — unfinished reservations persist across devices without holding items. Availability is checked again at submission; deleted reservations do not go to trash. [Cart and submission](/rezervacijos/rezervacijos), [resources](/rezervacijos/istekliai)
- ✨ **One meeting-completion rule** — meeting status, the automatic task, and agenda filters agree on completion, including the exceptions for informational and deferred items, breaks, and VU SR bodies. [Guide](/visak/darbotvarkes-klausimai#uzpildyta)
- ⭐ **Problems linked to discussions** — connect a problem to an agenda item and see the meetings where it was discussed. [Guide](/visak/problemos#posedziai)
- ✨ **What vusa.lt shows** — on a meeting, **What is shown?** next to **Visibility** explains what of the meeting is published and what never is. The **Files** tab reminds you that files are never shown on vusa.lt. [Guide](/visak/failai#viesumas)
- ⭐ **Revoke a file link** — an opened file is reachable through a link that needs no sign-in; you can now revoke it, and the old link stops working at once. [Guide](/visak/failai#atsaukti)
- ✨ **Assignment period manager** — adjust many assignments in one timeline, review suggested corrections, and use full screen. Unsaved changes remain distinct from saved dates. [Guide](/visak/pareigybiu-laikotarpiai)
- ⭐ **Private content recovery and both languages** — recover unfinished page and news edits from device or server copies. Edit both languages together, save and publish each separately, and avoid silently overwriting a colleague's newer version. [Recovery](/svetaine/puslapiai#atkurimas), [language versions](/svetaine/puslapiai#kalbu-versijos)
- ✨ **Separate types and simpler documents** — manage institution and duty types independently. Every member can browse the document archive; document managers control SharePoint synchronization and removal. [Types](/sistema/tipai), [documents](/visak/dokumentai)
- ⭐ **System maintenance from the application** — super administrators can run maintenance actions from System status; support requests let members report problems and follow their resolution. [System status](/sistema/sistemos-busena), [support](/sistema/pagalbos-uzklausos)
- ⭐ **Goals remain a pilot** — enabled units can connect goals, completed actions, problems, and agenda items and publish selected goals on their website. [Guide](/visak/tikslai)
- ✨ **Feature hints on phones** — on a phone or tablet without a mouse, a new feature's hint opens by itself once its button settles on screen. Scrolling past closes it, and the corner notch reopens it.
